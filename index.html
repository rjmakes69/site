Welcome!
Welcome to.. this. This is my website. To be honest, big social media apps suck. So, I decided to make a website. This is my website, which is under neverending construction.

At some point in march I stumbled across dvd3000's website, and I thought it was pretty cool. So I made my own, which is consecutively being hosted on two sites, neocities and aftersleep.

This is the amazing thing known as my website. I post things like updates, art (soon), and blog posts.

I update my site somewhat frequently, and I might change some things, and I might not. I don't have a definite update schedule, so don't expect it to change on a set time every day. I update it when I decide to, and that's that.
